The Science

Ingredient Explorer

Every active ingredient we use, explained. Understand the science behind your skincare and haircare routine.

Niacinamide

Vitamin B3 Powerhouse

Minimizes pores, evens skin tone, strengthens the skin barrier, and reduces fine lines. Works for all skin types and pairs well with most actives. Found in our Brightening Serum and Barrier Repair Cream.

Hyaluronic Acid

Deep Hydration

Holds up to 1000x its weight in water. Plumps skin, reduces fine lines, and creates a moisture reservoir. Our multi-molecular HA penetrates multiple skin layers for all-day hydration.

Retinol

Gold Standard Anti-Aging

Accelerates cell turnover, boosts collagen, fades dark spots, and smooths texture. Our encapsulated retinol formula minimizes irritation while maximizing efficacy.

Vitamin C

Brightening Antioxidant

Neutralizes free radicals, brightens complexion, boosts collagen synthesis, and protects against UV damage. Our stabilized L-Ascorbic Acid formula maintains potency.

Peptides

Cellular Communication

Signal skin cells to produce more collagen and elastin. Our peptide complex targets multiple signs of aging — firmness, elasticity, and texture — at the cellular level.

Keratin Complex

Hair Reconstruction

Rebuilds damaged hair structure from within. Our hydrolyzed keratin penetrates the cortex to repair broken bonds, restoring strength, shine, and manageability to all hair types.

Research

Understanding Beauty at the Deepest Level

True innovation begins with understanding — the biology of skin and hair, and the science of formulation.

01

Skin Biology

Skin is a complex organ with distinct layers, each requiring different care. Understanding the epidermis, dermis, and subcutaneous tissue allows us to formulate with molecular precision.

02

Hair Science

From the medulla through the cortex to the cuticle — hair structure determines treatment response. Our formulations interact at the structural level, not just the surface.

03

Scalp Microbiome

The scalp ecosystem's microbiome, pH balance, and sebaceous activity directly affect hair and skin health. We formulate to support, not disrupt, this delicate balance.

04

AI-Driven Research

Processing data on ingredient interactions, client results, and long-term performance — our AI identifies optimization opportunities that drive continuous product improvement.
